Why OneDrive for Linux

It runs the real OneDrive on the web, so sharing, Photos, Recycle bin and Office documents work as in a browser, in a desktop window with downloads, notifications and separate work and personal profiles. It works well next to a sync client. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0).
